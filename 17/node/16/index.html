艺术与科技论坛.

今天无论科学家还是艺术家，甚至其他任何领域，学科间的交叉越来越重要。技术是人体和感官的延伸，而艺术在某种程度上矫正各种感知的比例，最终形成一种“整体意识”。为了达成这种“整体意识”，艺术与科技论坛力邀科技界和艺术家代表，分享各自研究的领域，看似风马不接的交锋或许能打开彼此思维的边界。基金会也将长期推动艺术与科技的交流与合作，希望通过此次论坛为未来更深入的跨界耕耘打下基础。 本次论坛将以"互动.生态"为题深入交流艺术与科技交叉的方方面面包括： 交互映像作品、设计技术、数码产品、媒体装置艺术作品、软件、游戏等创意交互成果。交互已经融入我们的生活、娱乐、工作方方面面，对今天的设计和创意工作也产生着重大影响。

关键日期

  • 时间： 2017.6.9 号 （半天）

虚拟现实 ／增强现实论坛.
在这个信息时代，技术的飞速发展大大拓宽了我们对生活的想法以及对生活可能性的预期。特别是VR和AR技术的引入，使得“虚拟”技术改变着我们在生活中的“现实”。因此，“虚拟”与“现实”之间的界限越来越模糊，甚至是消失。这种跨界整合的现象表明，VR／AR技术已经成为新行业发展的趋势。在这个论坛上，我们将荣幸邀请来自包括艺术、工业以及学术等不同领域的代表来交流新想法、新技术以及新商业学问。本次论坛旨在为VR／AR领域的研究人员与从业人员提供一个相互交流的平台，共同展望未来发展可能性。

本论坛将讨论以下主题

  • VR / AR的理论基础、技术应用、生态系统和发展趋势;
  • 人机交互在提高VR / AR系统用户体验和稳健性方面所扮演的角色;
  • VR / AR在系统商业化中的主要挑战，如晕动症、海量三维内容的快速制作等;
  • VR / AR未来可能的技术改进及其对产业链的影响;
  • 将通过学术界和行业界的合作，促进行业领域的进步;
  • 论坛参与者可以从行业相关方和实质参与到这一领域的学术研究者中获得关于VR / AR研究的前瞻性的见解，还能了解到VR / AR未来的发展机会和挑战。参会者还能够与受邀的领域专家们进行深入互动，参与研讨会和小组讨论，从而深入的了解VR / AR生态系统的整体情况。

时间：2017.6.9号（上午）

Creativity, Innovation, and Entrepreneurship.

The North America is a huge and responsive market for both imported technology products and IT services. Whether you are doing, growing your business, or perhaps you are thinking about 'taking the leap' to the North America ChineseCHI 2017 invites you to this seminar and panel discussion series to help you understand the market, plan your approach, and suggest practical methods and alternatives for implementing your business and marketing plans. This event is designed to be a valuable offering to the Chinese entrepreneur community providing information on timely and relevant topics for business executives who is interested in entering the North America market. The seminar and panel discussion series consist of inviting active business leaders in sectors such as famed business accelerator, business consulting, university administrators, marketing and the lending industry to present useful tools and information.

Key topics

  • Global development trend of human-computer interaction, user experience and AI;
  • Setup of U.S. based incubators who can provide you with guidance, expertise, and resources to help your business grow; seed money and valuable connections to investors;
  • Intellectual resources provided by North America based educational entities;
  • Dealing with “border issues”, such as U.S. travel and immigration planning, U.S. business planning, taxation, currency exchange and legal considerations;
  • Understanding “logistics issues”, such as establishing a USA business identity and fulfillment using local resources;
  • “Sales/marketing strategies”, including market overview, sales/marketing fundamentals, lessons and pitfalls.

Attendees benefit from the insight of professionals who are substantively involved in the technology industry and maintain up-to-the-minute knowledge about trends and developments affecting your future decision. These events are structured in an interactive format which encourages questions from attendees and allows for feedback. An international entrepreneur-to-entrepreneur business networking will help Chinese entrepreneurs to recognize the potential that lies in dialoguing and collaborating with other international entrepreneurs who have gained success both domestically and internationally in the technology industry.

Virtual Reality ／ Augmented Reality.
In this information age, the advances of technologies greatly broaden our outlook of on life and its possibilities. Inparticular, “Virtual” experiences introduced by VR or AR technologies are changing the “Reality” we are living in. Boundaries between “Virtual” and“Reality” are increasingly blurred and even dissolved. Such trans-boundary integration indicate the trend of VR/AR becoming an emerging industry. In this forum, we will honorably invite representatives from different disciplines across art, industry, and academia to exchange new ideas, technologies and business wisdom. The forum aims to serve as a platform for researchers and practitioners in the area of VR/AR to interact and to envision future possibilities.

Key topics

  • Theoretical foundations, enabling techniques,ecosystem, and emerging trends of VR/AR;
  • The role of Human-Computer Interaction inimproving the user experience and robustiness of VR/AR systems;
  • Key challenges to the commercialization of VR/AR systems, such as Motion Sickness, rapid production of massive 3D contents, etc;
  • Potential technological improvements of VR/AR in the near future and their impact on the industrial chain;
  • Advancement brought in by collaboration andeven integration of academia and industry;
  • Forum attendees will gain insights not only about the cutting-edge VR/AR research but also about the prospect of the opportunities and challenges in VR/AR business, from industrial stakeholders and academic pioneers who have substantively involved in this area. Attendees will benefit from seminar talks, panel discussions, and in-depth interactions with the invited domain experts to get a holistic picture of the VR/AR ecosystem.

Intelligent Transportation and IoT.

The relationship between human, vehicle and road is getting more and more complex, and will lead to new driving and riding experience. In the future, the transportation issue will be more complicated. Whether driving activity could become easier to us, depends on the creative interaction styles based on the intelligent transportation technology. The "Intelligent transportation and IoT" forum of Chinese CHI 2017 will invite researchers and experts from industries and academia, to discuss the "pervasive" intelligent transportation and human-computer interaction based on the IoT (Internet of Things) technology.
