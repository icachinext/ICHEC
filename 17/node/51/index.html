Keynote Speakers

INAKAGE, Masa
庆应义塾大学传媒设计研究生院院长兼教授
庆应义塾大学庆应传媒设计研究院主任
庆应义塾大学与新加坡国立大学联合技术实现中心联合主任　

Masa Inakage是庆应义塾大学传媒设计研究生院（Keio University Graduate School of Media Design，KMD）的创始人、院长及教授。目前还担任由新加坡国立研究基金会资助的庆应义塾大学与新加坡国立大学联合技术实现中心的联合主任。他积极参与媒体设计、创意及生产负责以及战略设计的相关活动。Inakage利用他在娱乐设计、故事思考以及用户体验设计方面的专业知识，带领和开展各种学术和专业项目。这些项目所覆盖的范围非常广泛，从创新理念的学术探索、盈利性的创新产品及服务到非营利性的社会变革。他现任众多企业的创新战略咨询顾问委员会以及执行委员会的成员，曾任世界经济论坛、移动生活中心及网络博览会（Net Expo）等政府委员会和国际组织的成员。计算机图形学、动画制作、媒体艺术以及交互设计领域中，Inakage在期刊、学术研讨会、节日会演以及美术馆中发表了超过100件研究论文、艺术作品、动画作品以及交互设计作品。Inakage的作品已经在国际美术馆和节日会演中展出，包括纽约现代艺术博物馆和奥地利林茨的电子艺术中心。他目前的研究包括情感化城市、物联网、社会娱乐、集体创意、Storytelling、全球创意领导力以及创新设计战略。

INAKAGE, Masa
Dean and Professor, Keio University Graduate School of Media Design
Director, Keio University Keio Media Design Research Institute
Co-Director, Keio-NUS CUTE Center

Masa Inakage is the founding Dean and Professor of Keio University Graduate School of Media Design, known as Keio Media Design or KMD. He is currently the Co-Director of Keio-NUS CUTE Center that is supported by the National Research Foundation in Singapore. He has been active as media designer, creative director and producer, as well as strategist. Inakage leads and advises various academic and professional projects using his expertise in entertainment design, story thinking, and user experience design. Projects are diverse, ranging from academic exploration of innovative concepts, for-profit innovative products and services, to non-profit social change. He sits on advisory board and executive board of numerous corporations for corporate innovation strategy, as well as has served as members of government committees and international organizations including the World Economic Forum, Mobile Life Center, and NetExpo. He has published more than 100 research papers, artworks, animation works, and interaction design works in refereed journals, conferences, festivals, and art galleries in the areas of computer graphics, animation, media art, and interaction design. His works have been shown internationally at galleries and festivals, including Museum of Modern Art in NYC and Ars Electronica Center in Linz, Austria.

His current research interests include Affective City, post-Internet of Things, Social Entertainment, Collective Creativity, Storytelling, Creative Global Leadership, and Design Innovation Strategy.

童欣 　
微软亚洲研究院网络图形组首席研究员。

童欣博士现就职于微软亚洲研究院，任网络图形组的首席研究员。1999年童欣在清华大学获得计算机图形学博士学位。并分别于1993年和1996年获得了浙江大学计算机科学的学士学位和硕士学位。他的博士学位论文主要关于硬件加速体绘制。他对建模、渲染、纹理、图像等方面的研究比较感兴趣。具体来说，他的研究重点是不同材料光的相互作用和光的传输的基本原理，和开发高效的外观建模和渲染方法。此外他对图像捕捉和面部动画的研究也很感兴趣。

TONG, Xin
Principal Researcher, Internet Graphics Group of MSRA　

Xin Tong is now a principal researcher in Internet Graphics Group of Microsoft Research Asia. He obtained his Ph.D. degree in Computer Graphics from Tsinghua University in 1999. His Ph.D. thesis is about hardware assisted volume rendering. He got his B.S. Degree and Master Degree in Computer Science from Zhejiang University in 1993 and 1996 respectively.

His research interests include appearance modeling and rendering, texture synthesis, and image based modeling and rendering. Specifically, his research concentrates on studying the underline principles of material light interaction and light transport, and developing efficient methods for appearance modeling and rendering. He is also interested in performance capturing and facial animation.
