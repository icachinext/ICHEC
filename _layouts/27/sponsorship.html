Sponsorship Opportunities

Sponsor names appear in the official program and proceedings, with the sponsorship level. Logos are published on this website with a hyperlink. Sponsors may supply a limited amount of material for the registration kit. Each level includes the benefits of the preceding levels. Amounts are in the host currency and will be confirmed with the venue.

Bronze Sponsor — amount TBA

  • 1 complimentary conference registration
  • Acknowledged as a Coffee Break Sponsor

Silver Sponsor — amount TBA

Includes all Bronze benefits, plus:

  • 1 additional complimentary registration (2 total)
  • Acknowledged as a Lunch Break Sponsor
  • Company logo displayed at on-site registration
  • Opportunity to provide a brochure or handouts at registration

Gold Sponsor — amount TBA

Includes all Silver benefits, plus:

  • 1 additional complimentary registration (3 total)
  • Acknowledged as a Demo/Art Show Sponsor
  • Display space during the art show, demo, and poster sessions
  • Recognized as co-sponsor of the best paper awards

Platinum Sponsor — amount TBA

Includes all Gold benefits, plus:

  • 1 additional complimentary registration (4 total)
  • Acknowledged as gala dinner co-sponsor and/or overall conference sponsor
  • Special recognition during the event and in conference materials
