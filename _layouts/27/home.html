About ICHEC 2027

Welcome to the International Conference on Human-Engaged Computing (ICHEC 2027). ICHEC is a global platform for exploring how computing—especially Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies—is intertwined with human values, cultures, and lived experiences. Formerly known as Chinese CHI, the conference welcomes HCI researchers, practitioners, designers, and industry leaders from around the world.

The conference is tentatively scheduled for November 3–5, 2027, at Shanghai University of Engineering Science in Shanghai, China. The conference theme, keynote speakers, and submission deadlines will be announced on this page.
