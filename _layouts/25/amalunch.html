We are delighted to introduce the ICHEC 2025 Ask Me Anything (AMA) Student Mentoring Lunch Program!

The goal of this program is to provide early-career students and researchers with broader access to resources that will help them navigate the ICHEC community and their broader career in Human-Computer Interaction research domain. The primary objective is to offer opportunities for in-depth research discussions while fostering an environment where participants can connect with mentors and peers in a friendly and casual atmosphere.

This year's Student Mentoring Program will be held in-person as an AMA Student-Mentor Lunch Program during the conference lunch breaks on Nov 21st and 22nd. This is an in-depth mentoring opportunity for undergraduate, Master's and Ph.D. students, where you will have the chance to ask questions, discuss your ongoing research, or talk about career development opportunities with faculty mentors and senior HCI researchers.


Event Details

  • Time: During the lunch breaks on Day 1 and Day 2 of the ICHEC 2025 conference.
  • Format: We will match you with a preferred mentor based on your choices. You will have lunch with your mentor and 1-2 other students for an informal discussion lasting approximately one hour.

How to Apply

Participation in this program is by invitation only. Interested students must submit an application via the online form before the deadline.

  • Application Form: https://forms.gle/ap4epBJXbJ8VDrBY6

Required Information (via the online form):

  • Name
  • Email
  • Affiliation
  • Degree Program and Year (Bachelor, Master, or PhD, and current year of study)
  • Preferred Communication Language (English/Chinese)
  • Research Interests
  • CV (2-page maximum)
  • Benefits Statement: Please briefly describe what you hope to gain from this program
  • Question for Mentor: "What question would you ask your mentor during lunch?"

Important Dates

  • Application Deadline: November 7, 2025
  • Notification of Decision: November 14, 2025

Selection Process & Important Information

Selection Process: After the deadline, all applications will be reviewed by the Student Mentoring Program committee. Our goal is to support students who will benefit most from this opportunity. The selection process will prioritize these applicants while also aiming for diversity in student backgrounds, research topics, and geographic locations.

Seat Allocation: To ensure diversity, we may limit the number of accepted students from the same institution or research advisor.

Confidentiality: All your application materials will be kept strictly confidential and will only be used for the review and organization of this program.

Important Note: The Student Mentoring Program is a part of the ICHEC 2025 conference. All invited participants are responsible for their own conference registration, travel, and accommodation costs.

我们很高兴推出 ICHEC 2025 Ask Me Anything (AMA) 学生导师午餐计划！

本计划的目标是为职业生涯早期的学生和研究人员提供更广泛的资源，帮助他们融入ICHEC社区，并在人机交互研究领域发展更广阔的职业生涯。我们将为参与者提供深入研究讨论的机会，同时营造一个友好、轻松的氛围，让参与者可以与导师和同行建立联系。

今年的学生导师计划将于11月21日和22日的会议午餐时段，以AMA 学生-导师午餐计划的形式线下举行。这是一个为本科生、硕士生和博士生提供的深入指导机会，届时你将有机会向学术导师和资深的人机交互研究人员提问、讨论你正在进行的研究或职业发展机会。


活动详情

  • 时间：ICHEC 2025 会议第一天与第二天，午餐时段。
  • 形式：我们将根据你的选择，为你匹配一位心仪的导师。你将与导师以及其他 1-2 位同学共进午餐，进行约一小时的自由交流。

报名方式

本次活动为邀请制。感兴趣的同学需要在截止日期前，通过下方的在线问卷提交报名申请。

  • 报名链接： https://forms.gle/ap4epBJXbJ8VDrBY6

报名所需信息（在线问卷）：

  • 姓名
  • 邮箱
  • 所属机构
  • 学位项目和年份（学士/硕士/博士，以及目前就读年份）
  • 沟通语言（英语/中文）
  • 研究方向
  • 个人简历（不超过2页）
  • 参会理由陈述：请简要说明你期望从本次活动中获得什么帮助
  • 给导师的问题："午餐时，你最想问导师什么问题？"

重要日期

  • 申请截止日期：2025年11月7日
  • 录取通知日期：2025年11月14日

选拔流程与须知

选拔流程：报名截止后，学生导师计划委员会将对所有申请进行审核。我们希望能借此机会帮助到最需要指导的同学，因此选拔过程将优先考虑那些能从本次活动中获益最多的申请者。同时，我们也会尽力确保参与学生的背景、研究方向和地域分布的多样性。

席位分配：为确保多样性，我们可能会限制来自同一机构或同一导师的入选学生数量。

信息保密：我们将严格保密您的所有申请材料。您的个人信息仅会用于本次活动的审核与安排，绝不会向第三方泄露。

重要须知：学生导师计划是 ICHEC 2025 会议的一部分，所有受邀参加者需自行负责会议的注册、差旅及住宿费用。
