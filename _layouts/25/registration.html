Registration

Welcome to the registration page for ICHEC 2025.

The early registration deadline is October 29, 2025. Regular and student rates apply, with fees increasing after this date. Please register early to take advantage of the discounted rates.

Note: All registration deadlines are by the end of day (EOD) Singapore Time (GMT+8).

Please ensure that pop-ups are enabled in your browser before you begin registration and read each section carefully. All fees are listed in Singapore Dollars (S$) and are inclusive of the 9% Singapore Goods and Services Tax (GST). Minor rounding differences (up to one cent) may occur due to GST computation.

1. Authors of Accepted FULL Papers

At least one author of each accepted FULL (long) paper and at least one organiser of each workshop must complete a Standard registration by October 29, 2025.

Each accepted full (long) paper must be covered by a distinct Standard registration. The same registrant cannot use a single Standard registration to cover multiple full papers.

Note: This rule applies only to full (long) papers. Short papers and other accepted submissions (e.g., posters, demos) do not require a Standard registration, but must still register, or their contribution will be withdrawn.

2. Refund Policy

  1. Author registrations are non-refundable.
  2. The Student registration type is not valid to cover a FULL paper.
  3. A 50% refund may be granted for cancellation requests submitted before November 9, 2025, 11:59 PM Singapore Time (GMT+8).
  4. All refund requests must be submitted via email to ichec@smu.edu.sg for consideration.
  5. Approved refunds will be processed approximately 3 months after the conference.

3. Inclusions

Your registration includes:

  • Access to the conference for all 3 days: Friday, November 21, 2025 to Sunday, November 23, 2025.
  • Lunches and coffee breaks for all 3 days.
  • Attendance at the Banquet Dinner on Saturday, November 22, 2025.


Venue and Accommodations

1. Venue

ICHEC 2025 will take place in the heart of Singapore at the Singapore Management University (SMU).

  • Day 1 (November 21, 2025): School of Computing and Information Systems 2 (School of Economics), 90 Stamford Road. Google Map | Baidu Map
  • Days 2 & 3 (November 22–23, 2025): SMU Administration Building, 81 Victoria Street. Google Map | Baidu Map

Both venues are centrally located in Singapore’s civic and cultural district, with easy access to public transportation, hotels, and dining options.

2. Recommended Hotel

We have secured corporate rates at the nearby Hotel Royal Queens (12 Queen Street, Singapore 188553). To reserve a room, please send a booking enquiry to sharon@royalqueens.com.sg with the subject line ICHEC 2025. The hotel is a 6-minute walk to the Day 1 venue and a 5-minute walk to the Days 2–3 venue.
