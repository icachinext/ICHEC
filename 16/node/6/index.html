Sponsorship Opportunities

On behalf of the organizing committee we invite you to be a sponsor of the 4th International Symposium of Chinese CHI (Chinese CHI 2016), and thus play an essential role for the success of the conference.

A rich technical program that covers all the areas of human computer interaction - plus invited talks by leading researchers in the field, co-located with CHI 2016 conference and many HCI workshops, make Chinese CHI 2016 one of the unique events for Chinese HCI researchers, practitioners, educators and everyone who are interested in Chinese related HCI research and problems. This event is held in San Jose, CA, USA, a world-class center of emerging trends in culture, technology and design.

Your sponsorship is key to the success of Chinese CHI 2016 and your presence would contribute to the prestige and impact of this event and would motivate other sponsors to join as well.
