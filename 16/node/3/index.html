Registration

Registration
Chinese CHI 2016 is associated with CHI 2016 as one of its workshops. You may register for Chinese CHI through the CHI 2016 Attendee Registration website.  To register for Chinese CHI, you must also register for at least one day of the CHI conference. One-day registration for CHI is $300 for ACM or SIGCHI members or $375 for non-members if registered by *March 14, 2016* (early registration deadline). 

Please also make sure that you choose the right workshop (W39: Chinese CHI 2016 Symposium) in the registration site. The workshop access code for Chinese CHI is accessW39.

Visas
If you require an invitation letter for a visa to attend ChineseChi2016, you can contact tuhuawei09@gmail.com, or request a visa support letter from ACM if you also plan to attend CHI2016 (https://chi2016.acm.org/wp/registration/).

Purchasing Banquet Tickets
Chinese CHI 2016 will host a banquet from 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm on May 8th, 2016 at Mosaic Restaurant and Lounge in San Jose. The banquet ticket is not included in the conference registration. Please visit here to purchase tickets. The banquet ticket is $25 for a student attendee and $35 for a non-student attendee. You will need your CHI 2016 registration reference number to purchase tickets.
